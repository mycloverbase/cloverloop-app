The Creator plan is recommended for creators who want unlimited product listings without a monthly plan price. Free is the zero-cost entry point, while Pro Creator is intended for established, higher-volume creators.

| Plan | Monthly price | Platform transaction fee | Product listings |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Free | ₹0 | 0% | 1 paid and 5 free products per account lifetime |
| Creator | ₹0 | 4%; no monthly Gross Sales limit | Unlimited paid and free products |
| Pro Creator | ₹2,999 | 0% | Unlimited paid and free products |

Free has no CloverLoop platform transaction fee. Creator has a 4% platform transaction fee, and Pro Creator has a 0% platform transaction fee with a monthly subscription.

## Payment provider charges

Razorpay or another payment provider may charge processing fees under its own terms. Those charges are payable separately by the creator and are not included in CloverLoop's platform commission.

Payment-provider processing charges are set by Razorpay or another provider, are payable separately by creators, and are not included in CloverLoop's platform commission.
