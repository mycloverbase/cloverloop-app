## The short answer

CloverLoop is a platform for creators to sell digital products through a creator storefront. Its platform commission is separate from payment processing: Free is 0%, Creator is 4% before GST and Pro Creator is 3% before GST. With 18% GST on commission, the effective CloverLoop rates are 0%, 4.72% and 3.54%.

Payhip's [official pricing page](https://payhip.com/pricing) lists three plans in US dollars: Free at 5%, Plus at US$29/month plus 2%, and Pro at US$99/month plus 0% Payhip transaction fee. Stripe or PayPal processing fees remain additional. Payhip's percentage fees are shown as provider-listed rates; its pricing materials do not establish an India-specific GST-inclusive rate.

Compare the monthly subscription, currency conversion and applicable taxes alongside the percentage. See [CloverLoop pricing](/pricing), the [pricing and platform-fee guide](/help-center/pricing-and-platform-fees/understanding-cloverloop-pricing), and [frequently asked questions](/faq).

## How the plans behave as sales grow

Payhip lets sellers exchange a higher monthly subscription for a lower listed percentage: Free is 5%, Plus is 2% for US$29/month, and Pro is 0% for US$99/month. Stripe or PayPal processing remains separate on each plan.

CloverLoop uses a different structure. Free has no platform commission but limits the catalogue to one paid product and five free product listings. Creator supports unlimited products at 4% before GST, with no monthly subscription. Pro Creator lowers the rate to 3% before GST and adds a ₹5,000 monthly subscription before GST. ## Who should choose which platform?

Choose CloverLoop if you are an India-focused creator who prefers INR plan pricing, wants a 0% limited Free plan, or wants unlimited products without a monthly subscription on Creator. Compare the 4.72% Creator or 3.54% Pro Creator effective commission, Razorpay processing and any subscription GST.

Choose Payhip if unlimited products on its free plan, its Stripe/PayPal ecosystem, memberships or courses, or its stated EU/UK VAT handling better fit your business. Pro may suit sellers whose volume justifies US$99/month in exchange for a 0% Payhip transaction fee.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Payhip have a 0% transaction-fee plan?

Yes. Payhip lists Pro at US$99/month with a 0% Payhip transaction fee. Stripe/PayPal processing fees still apply.

### Is Payhip Free actually free?

Payhip lists no monthly subscription for Free, but it charges a 5% transaction fee in addition to payment-processor fees.

### Does CloverLoop offer unlimited products on Free?

No. CloverLoop Free includes one paid product and five free product listings. Creator and Pro Creator include unlimited paid and free products.

### Can an Indian creator use Payhip?

Payhip's checkout depends on supported payment processors such as Stripe and PayPal. Confirm that you can open and receive payouts through a supported account in your country before relying on it.
