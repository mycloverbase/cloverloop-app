Cookies are small files stored by a website in a browser. Websites may also use local storage, session storage, pixels, tags and similar technologies. CloverLoop may use these technologies to keep the Platform working, protect accounts, remember preferences and understand website usage.

## 1. Categories of technologies

| Category | Purpose | Examples / provider | Consent approach |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strictly necessary | Authentication, session continuity, security, checkout and remembering privacy choices. | Supabase authentication/session storage; CloverLoop consent preference; Razorpay checkout technologies when invoked. | Necessary for requested service. |
| Analytics | Understand page views, navigation, product interest, performance and aggregate usage. | Google Analytics 4 through Google Tag Manager, if enabled. | Optional analytics should remain off until required consent is obtained. |
| Marketing / advertising | Measure campaigns, attribute conversions, build audiences or personalise ads. | Google advertising tags or Meta Pixel, if enabled. | Optional technologies are used only after valid marketing consent where required. |
| Third-party checkout | Complete payment, prevent fraud and maintain payment-provider session state. | Razorpay and integrated payment methods. | Triggered when user proceeds to payment; provider policy also applies. |

## 2. Analytics and tag management

CloverLoop may use Google Tag Manager to deploy Google Analytics 4 and related measurement tags. The presence of a tag does not mean it should run before consent. Optional analytics begin only if you select “Allow analytics” on the cookie banner.

Google Tag Manager is not itself a cookie; it is a tag-management tool that can load other scripts. Actual cookies and storage entries are set by the tags and services configured through it.

## 3. Managing your choices

CloverLoop's cookie banner lets you reject optional analytics or allow analytics. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage. You can also block or delete cookies through browser settings. Blocking necessary cookies or session storage may prevent sign-in, checkout or other core features.
