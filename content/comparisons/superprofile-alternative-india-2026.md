## What Is SuperProfile and How Does It Work?

[SuperProfile](https://superprofile.bio/about) is a creator commerce platform operated by Cosmofeed Technologies Private Limited, an Indian company founded in 2021. It began as a link-in-bio tool and now covers much more of a creator's business.

- **Link-in-bio store:** one page that holds your products, links and lead forms.
- **Instagram AutoDM:** a creator sets a keyword, and anyone who comments it receives an automatic DM, usually with a link. The free plan covers Reels and posts. Stories and Lives sit on the Creator plan.
- **Digital products, courses, bookings, events and communities:** all sold from one account.
- **Scheduling and engagement tools:** Instagram and Facebook scheduling, AI replies and comment moderation, listed under Creator.
- **Payments in India:** buyers pay through SuperProfile's own checkout. You complete KYC instead of opening a separate payment gateway account, and earnings settle to your bank account on a T+1 basis.

The strengths are breadth, free AutoDM on Starter, no gateway setup and quick settlement. Those matter most to creators whose audience and sales both start on Instagram.
