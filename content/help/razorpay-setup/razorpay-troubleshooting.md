This article offers general Razorpay account guidance. For account-specific payment or settlement questions, use the relevant provider's official support channels.

## Account registration or KYC is not progressing

Check status and pending actions in the Razorpay Dashboard. Confirm submitted details are accurate and respond to requests for additional information. Contact Razorpay for account verification issues.

## Razorpay account is not activated

Review account activation status and any restrictions shown by Razorpay. CloverLoop cannot approve a Razorpay merchant account on Razorpay's behalf.

## Payment status

Check the payment status and transaction record directly with the provider that processed it. Never share passwords, OTPs or secret keys.

## Payment failed

Ask the customer to check the status with their bank or payment provider. Check the transaction in the provider's dashboard before attempting another payment.

## Settlement is missing or differs from the payment

Review settlement and transaction records in Razorpay, including fees, taxes, refunds and adjustments. Contact Razorpay about settlement ledger or bank-credit issues.
