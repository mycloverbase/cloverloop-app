## The short answer

CloverLoop is a platform for creators to sell digital products through a creator storefront. Its platform commission is separate from payment processing: the rates are 0% on Free, 4% on Creator and 3% on Pro Creator, with 18% GST applied to commission. Gumroad charges a percentage plus a fixed fee for direct sales, and a higher fee for sales through its Discover marketplace.

Gumroad's [official pricing](https://gumroad.com/pricing) lists 10% + US$0.50 per direct sale, with standard payment-processing fees additional. Its [fee help page](https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees) lists standard card processing at 2.9% + US$0.30; exact payment-method and country terms can vary. Discover marketplace sales incur a 30% fee that includes processing. Gumroad also acts as Merchant of Record for applicable indirect-tax collection and remittance. CloverLoop's plan fees are in INR and Razorpay processing charges are additional.

The right fit depends on whether you value a percentage-based storefront fee or Gumroad's marketplace and tax-administration services. See [CloverLoop pricing](/pricing), the [pricing and platform-fee guide](/help-center/pricing-and-platform-fees/understanding-cloverloop-pricing), and [frequently asked questions](/faq).

## Pricing and platform fees

CloverLoop's commission rates are listed before GST. At 18% GST, its effective commission rates are 0%, 4.72% and 3.54%. The Pro Creator subscription is ₹5,000/month before GST. Razorpay processing charges are separate, and CloverLoop invoices its commission and applicable GST monthly.

Gumroad's [official pricing](https://gumroad.com/pricing) lists 10% + US$0.50 for direct/profile sales. Its [fee help page](https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees) lists card-processing costs for direct sales, while Discover sales have a 30% fee that includes processing. Gumroad says it acts as Merchant of Record for indirect taxes. Its published fee is denominated in USD, so the fixed amount and any currency conversion affect the final cost.

## Who should choose which platform?

Choose CloverLoop if you mainly sell downloadable products, want INR plan pricing, and prefer a percentage-based fee structure. Free has a limited catalogue and no commission; Creator has no monthly subscription and unlimited paid and free products; Pro Creator lowers the commission and adds a monthly subscription.

Choose Gumroad if Merchant of Record tax handling, international selling infrastructure, memberships or marketplace discovery are important to your business. Those services may justify a higher direct-sale fee or a 30% Discover fee. This is not a like-for-like feature comparison.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Gumroad charge a monthly subscription?

No. Gumroad's official pricing page says there are no monthly charges; fees apply per transaction.

### What does Gumroad charge for direct sales?

Gumroad lists 10% + US$0.50 per direct/profile transaction. Standard direct-sale payment-processing fees are additional.

### What is Gumroad Discover's fee?

Gumroad lists a 30% fee for Discover marketplace sales and says it includes processing.

### Is CloverLoop always cheaper than Gumroad?

No platform is cheapest for every creator. Compare CloverLoop's GST-inclusive commission and Razorpay processing with Gumroad's fixed USD fee, direct-sale processing, Discover fee, currency conversion and tax-administration service.

### Does CloverLoop handle international sales tax?

Creators should check their own tax obligations and applicable cross-border rules. Gumroad's Merchant of Record service has a different tax-administration role.
