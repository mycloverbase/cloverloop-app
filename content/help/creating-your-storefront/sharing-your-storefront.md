Your storefront works best when your audience can find it easily. Use the same primary storefront link across the channels where you publish content.

## Instagram

Add the storefront URL to your profile link. Mention relevant products in captions and direct interested followers to the link in your bio.

## YouTube

Add the storefront URL to relevant video descriptions and channel profile links. Explain which product relates to the video.

## WhatsApp

Share the link with people who have asked for the product or opted to receive updates. Avoid unsolicited bulk messaging.

## Website and newsletter

Add a clear Store or Digital Products link to your website navigation and relevant newsletter content.

## Set clear expectations

Tell people what they will find when they open the link. Use accurate descriptions and do not promise content that is not included.
