CloverLoop publishes three plans. The plan determines the platform transaction fee and the number of paid and free product listings available to the creator.

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Monthly price | Platform transaction fee | Product listings and Gross Sales |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | ₹0 | 0% | 1 paid product and 5 free products per account lifetime |
| Creator | ₹0 | 4% | Unlimited paid and free products; no monthly Gross Sales limit |
| Pro Creator | ₹2,999 | 0% | Unlimited paid and free products |

Free has no CloverLoop platform transaction fee. Creator has a 4% platform transaction fee, and Pro Creator has a 0% platform transaction fee with a ₹2,999 monthly subscription. Platform rates and monthly subscription prices are exclusive of applicable GST where required.

## Payment provider charges

Razorpay or another payment provider may charge processing fees under its own terms. Those charges are payable separately by the creator and are not included in CloverLoop's platform commission.

## Choosing a plan

Free is the zero-price entry point for a small catalogue, with no platform transaction fee and one paid and five free product listings per account lifetime. Creator is the recommended no-monthly-fee option for unlimited listings at a 4% platform fee. Pro Creator costs ₹2,999/month and has no platform transaction fee.
