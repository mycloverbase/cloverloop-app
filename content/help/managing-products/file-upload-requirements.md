Prepare clear, reliable files for your digital product. Product files are private and are not available to storefront visitors as downloads yet.

## Prepare your files

Use clear filenames, remove temporary files and confirm that each file opens correctly on a typical device.

## Choose a suitable file size

Files must be larger than 0 bytes and no larger than 100 MB. Consider whether compressing a large file would affect its usability or quality.

## Supported formats

Supported formats are PDF, MP3, DOC, DOCX, PPT, PPTX, XLS, XLSX, EPUB, ZIP, TXT, RTF, MP4, WAV, JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP. The uploaded file's extension, reported type and size are checked before it can be saved as a product.

## Final quality check

Open the exact file you intend to upload. Check that it is complete, readable and free of unintended personal or confidential information.
