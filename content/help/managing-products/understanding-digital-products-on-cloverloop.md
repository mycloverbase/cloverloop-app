Digital products are files such as guides, templates or audio resources. Creators can add and manage product listings in the dashboard and display active listings on their public storefront. Product files are stored privately and are not exposed as public download links.

The current storefront release is a pre-payment catalogue. Visitors can see product details and prices, but purchase and download controls are disabled while payment and secure delivery are being prepared.

## Examples

- PDFs and ebooks
- Guitar tabs and sheet music
- Design templates and printable resources
- Presets and digital creative assets
- Guides, worksheets and other downloadable files

## Choose a useful product

A good digital product solves a specific problem or helps a customer achieve a clear outcome. Explain what is included, who it is for and what software or equipment may be required.

## Respect intellectual property

Only upload material you created or are authorised to distribute. Do not sell copyrighted work, third-party assets or personal information without the necessary rights and permissions.
