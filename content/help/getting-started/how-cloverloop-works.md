CloverLoop helps independent creators sell digital products through a dedicated online storefront. Creators can share their storefront with their audience and sell downloadable work directly.

## Your creator storefront

Each creator has a dedicated storefront address, for example cloverloop.in/creatorname. It presents the creator's profile and the digital products they choose to offer. Creators can share this link through social media, messaging apps, email and their own websites.

## Products and customers

Creators prepare digital files, add product information and set a price or make a product free. Customers visit the storefront, review product information and follow the available purchase or download flow.

## How payments work

A creator connects their own Razorpay account. When a customer makes a paid purchase, Razorpay processes the payment through the creator's account. Eligible funds are settled by Razorpay to the creator's registered bank account, subject to Razorpay's terms and settlement process.

## How CloverLoop charges

CloverLoop calculates platform transaction commission on successful paid transactions and invoices the creator separately each month. Commission is not deducted from the customer's checkout payment. Razorpay processing fees are separate. Free products do not attract CloverLoop transaction commission.

## The creator journey

Creator creates an account → configures a storefront → adds products → shares the storefront → customer purchases → Razorpay processes payment → customer receives the digital product → Razorpay settles eligible funds to the creator.

## Next steps

Start with the account setup guide, review the storefront guide, prepare your product files and read the Razorpay setup guide before accepting paid orders.
