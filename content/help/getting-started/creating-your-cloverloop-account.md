Create a CloverLoop account using the registration options currently displayed on the website.

## Register with email

Open the sign-up page and enter the requested account details. Use an email address you can access because verification and account communications may be sent there.

## Register with Google

If Google sign-in is enabled, choose that option, select the Google account you want to use and complete authentication.

## Verify your email

If CloverLoop requests email verification, open the verification email and follow its instructions. Check spam or junk folders if it does not arrive.

## Sign in and recover access

Use the login page to access your account. If you cannot remember your password, use the password-reset option and follow the email instructions.

## Keep your account secure

Use a unique password, protect your email account and do not share one-time codes or authentication links.
