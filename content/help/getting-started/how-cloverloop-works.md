CloverLoop is designed around helping independent creators plan how to present digital products through a dedicated online storefront.

## The creator storefront concept

A creator storefront brings a creator's identity and digital-product information together in a shareable destination. Creators can plan how to describe their work and where a storefront link would fit across their channels.

## Published plans

Free is ₹0/month, with 0% on the first ₹10,000 in Gross Sales per calendar month and 4% only on incremental Gross Sales above that amount. The allowance resets on the first day of each calendar month (IST), and sales are not stopped after it is used. Free includes one paid and five free product listings per account lifetime.

Creator is ₹0/month with a 4% platform commission, unlimited paid and free product listings, and no monthly Gross Sales limit. Pro Creator is ₹1,499/month with a 2.5% platform commission, unlimited paid and free product listings, and no monthly Gross Sales limit.

## Payment provider charges

Razorpay or another payment provider may charge processing fees under its own terms. Those charges are payable separately by the creator and are not included in CloverLoop's platform commission.

The Free-plan 4% platform commission applies only to incremental Gross Sales above ₹10,000, not to the full month's Gross Sales.
