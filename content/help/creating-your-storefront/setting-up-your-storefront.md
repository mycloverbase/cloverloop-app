A complete storefront helps visitors understand who you are and what you create. Prepare the following information before configuring your page.

## Creator name and profile image

Use the name your audience recognises and a clear profile image or brand mark.

## Short description

Explain what you create and who your products are for. Keep it specific and concise.

## Storefront URL

Choose a short, memorable URL that is easy to share. Avoid confusing spelling.

## Product presentation

Use descriptive titles, accurate descriptions and suitable cover images. Make the contents and intended use of each product clear.

## Review before sharing

Check spelling, links, prices and product details. Preview the storefront on desktop and mobile if preview functionality is available.
