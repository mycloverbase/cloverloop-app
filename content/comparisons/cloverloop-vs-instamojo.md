## The short answer

CloverLoop is a platform for creators to sell digital products through a creator storefront. Its platform commission is separate from payment processing: 0% on Free, 4% on Creator and 3% on Pro Creator before GST. Applying 18% GST to CloverLoop commission gives effective rates of 0%, 4.72% and 3.54%.

Instamojo's [current official Online Store pricing page](https://www.instamojo.com/pricing/) lists Lite at 5% + ₹3, Starter at 5% + ₹3, and Growth at 2% + ₹3. It states that GST is charged in addition to the listed prices. The page reviewed does not show a separate current digital-product fee schedule. Older online calculator figures of 10% + ₹3 and 5% + ₹3 are not used as current pricing.

Instamojo may suit sellers who need its broader ecommerce workflow, while CloverLoop's plans are focused on digital-product storefronts. See [CloverLoop pricing](/pricing), the [pricing and platform-fee guide](/help-center/pricing-and-platform-fees/understanding-cloverloop-pricing), and [frequently asked questions](/faq).

## Pricing and platform fees

CloverLoop lists commission before GST. Its effective commission including 18% GST is 0% for Free, 4.72% for Creator and 3.54% for Pro Creator. Pro Creator also has a ₹5,000/month subscription before GST. Razorpay processing fees are separate, and CloverLoop invoices commission and applicable GST monthly.

Instamojo's current official Online Store page lists a percentage plus ₹3 transaction fee for each plan and says GST is additional. The page lists a separate subscription for Starter and Growth. Confirm the current plan, tax and fee breakdown in the live checkout.

## Who should choose which platform?

Choose CloverLoop if you primarily sell ebooks, PDFs, study notes, templates, music downloads, presets or other digital files. Free offers a limited catalogue with no platform commission. Creator supports unlimited products without a monthly subscription at 4% before GST. Pro Creator lowers the rate to 3% before GST and adds a ₹5,000 monthly subscription.

Choose Instamojo if you also need a broader online-store workflow for physical goods, shipping or other ecommerce operations. Its current Online Store pricing lists percentage-plus-fixed fees and paid plan options; compare the tax and transaction details displayed for your own products and account.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does Instamojo currently list for transaction fees?

Its official Online Store pricing page lists Lite and Starter at 5% + ₹3, and Growth at 2% + ₹3. It says GST is charged in addition to those prices.

### Does Instamojo publish a separate current digital-product fee?

The official pricing page reviewed for this article shows Online Store plan rates rather than a separate digital-goods fee schedule. Confirm the fee for your product type in the current checkout.

### Does CloverLoop charge a fixed ₹3 per transaction?

No. CloverLoop's platform commission is percentage-based: 0%, 4% or 3% before GST depending on plan. Razorpay processing charges are separate.

### Which platform is cheaper for a digital download?

It depends on the plan, product price, monthly sales, subscription and payment processing. Compare CloverLoop's effective commission including GST with Instamojo's percentage-plus-fixed fee and applicable GST; no single plan is cheapest for every seller.
