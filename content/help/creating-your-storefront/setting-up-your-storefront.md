Clear creator and product information helps visitors understand who you are and what you create. Use this checklist to plan a storefront.

## Creator name and profile image

Use the name your audience recognises and a clear profile image or brand mark.

## Short description

Explain what you create and who your products are for. Keep it specific and concise.

## Creator name

Choose a name that is short, memorable and easy to recognise. Confirm account-specific naming options after signing in.

## Product presentation

Use descriptive titles, accurate descriptions and suitable cover images. Make the contents and intended use of each product clear.

## Review before publishing or sharing

Check spelling, links, prices and product details. Review your storefront on desktop and mobile before sharing it with an audience.
