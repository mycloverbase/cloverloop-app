CloverLoop publishes three plans. The plan determines the platform transaction fee and the number of paid and free product listings available to the creator.

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Monthly price | Platform transaction fee | Product listings and Gross Sales |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | ₹0 | 0% on the first ₹10,000 Gross Sales per calendar month; 4% only on incremental Gross Sales above ₹10,000 | 1 paid product and 5 free products per account lifetime |
| Creator | ₹0 | 4% | Unlimited paid and free products; no monthly Gross Sales limit |
| Pro Creator | ₹1,499 | 2.5% | Unlimited paid and free products; no monthly Gross Sales limit |

For Free, the ₹10,000 Gross Sales allowance resets on the first day of each calendar month (IST). Sales are not stopped after the allowance is used. A 4% platform commission applies only to Gross Sales above the allowance, not to the full month's Gross Sales. For example, at ₹15,000 in monthly Gross Sales, the first ₹10,000 is at 0% and the incremental ₹5,000 is charged at 4% (₹200 before applicable GST).

## Payment provider charges

Razorpay or another payment provider may charge processing fees under its own terms. Those charges are payable separately by the creator and are not included in CloverLoop's platform commission.

## Choosing a plan

Free is the zero-cost entry point for a small catalogue, with one paid and five free product listings per account lifetime and a ₹10,000 monthly Gross Sales allowance. Creator is the recommended no-monthly-fee option for unlimited listings and no monthly Gross Sales limit. Pro Creator is for established, higher-volume creators who prefer a 2.5% rate and a ₹1,499 monthly price.
