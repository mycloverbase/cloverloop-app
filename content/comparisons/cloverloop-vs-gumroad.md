## The short answer

CloverLoop is designed around creator storefronts for digital products. Its published plans are Free at ₹0/month with 0% on the first ₹10,000 Gross Sales per calendar month and 4% only on incremental Gross Sales above that allowance; Creator is ₹0/month at 4%; Pro Creator is ₹1,499/month at 2.5%. Payment-provider charges are separate from CloverLoop's platform commission.

Gumroad's [official pricing](https://gumroad.com/pricing) lists 10% + US$0.50 per direct sale, with standard payment-processing fees additional. Its [fee help page](https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees) lists standard card processing at 2.9% + US$0.30; exact payment-method and country terms can vary. Discover marketplace sales incur a 30% fee that includes processing. Gumroad also acts as Merchant of Record for applicable indirect-tax collection and remittance. CloverLoop plan pricing is in INR; Razorpay or other payment-provider processing charges are separate.

The right fit depends on whether you value a percentage-based storefront fee or Gumroad's marketplace and tax-administration services. See [CloverLoop pricing](/pricing), the [pricing and platform-fee guide](/help-center/pricing-and-platform-fees/understanding-cloverloop-pricing), and [frequently asked questions](/faq).

## Who should choose which platform?

Choose CloverLoop if you mainly sell downloadable products and want INR plan pricing. Free has a limited catalogue and a ₹10,000 monthly Gross Sales allowance; Creator has no monthly subscription and unlimited listings; Pro Creator is ₹1,499/month with a 2.5% fee.

Choose Gumroad if Merchant of Record tax handling, international selling infrastructure, memberships or marketplace discovery are important to your business. Those services may justify a higher direct-sale fee or a 30% Discover fee. This is not a like-for-like feature comparison.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Gumroad charge a monthly subscription?

No. Gumroad's official pricing page says there are no monthly charges; fees apply per transaction.

### What does Gumroad charge for direct sales?

Gumroad lists 10% + US$0.50 per direct/profile transaction. Standard direct-sale payment-processing fees are additional.

### What is Gumroad Discover's fee?

Gumroad lists a 30% fee for Discover marketplace sales and says it includes processing.

### Is CloverLoop always cheaper than Gumroad?

No platform is cheapest for every creator. Compare CloverLoop's plan rates, Free-plan monthly allowance and separate payment-provider charges with Gumroad's fixed USD fee, direct-sale processing, Discover fee, currency conversion and tax-administration service.

### Does CloverLoop handle international sales tax?

Creators should check their own tax obligations and applicable cross-border rules. Gumroad's Merchant of Record service has a different tax-administration role.
