CloverLoop offers three plans. The plan determines the platform transaction fee and the number of paid and free product listings available to the creator.

## Plan comparison

Free: ₹0 monthly subscription, 0% platform transaction fee, one paid product listing and five free product listings per account. Creator: ₹0 monthly subscription, 4% platform transaction fee (excluding GST) and unlimited paid and free product listings. Pro Creator: ₹5,000 monthly subscription (excluding GST), 3% platform transaction fee (excluding GST) and unlimited paid and free product listings.

GST at 18% is applied to CloverLoop's platform commission. The effective commission including GST is 0% for Free, 4.72% for Creator and 3.54% for Pro Creator. The monthly subscription prices are also shown excluding GST.

## What the transaction fee applies to

Platform transaction commission applies to successful paid transactions. Free products do not incur platform transaction commission. Razorpay processing fees are separate.

## How CloverLoop bills

CloverLoop calculates applicable commission monthly and invoices the creator separately, including applicable GST. The commission is not deducted from the customer's checkout payment.

## Razorpay fees are separate

Razorpay processing charges are additional and governed by the creator's Razorpay account terms.

## Creator plan example

If monthly successful paid sales total ₹1,00,000, a 4% platform transaction fee equals ₹4,000 before GST, plus ₹720 GST at 18%, for a ₹4,720 commission invoice allocation. This excludes Razorpay processing fees.

## Pro Creator example

If monthly successful paid sales total ₹5,00,000, a 3% platform transaction fee equals ₹15,000 before GST, plus ₹2,700 GST at 18%. Add the ₹5,000 monthly subscription, which is listed excluding GST. Razorpay processing fees are additional.

## Choosing a plan

Compare expected paid sales and the listing limits you need. Consider the monthly subscription and expected sales, not only the headline percentage.
