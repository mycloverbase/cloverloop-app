CloverLoop's payment model is designed around each creator's own Razorpay account. This keeps the creator's payment processing and settlement relationship directly with the payment provider.

## Customer checkout

A customer selects a paid product on the creator's storefront and follows the available checkout process.

## Payment processing

Razorpay processes the transaction through the creator's connected account. Payment confirmation must be received and verified before the product is treated as paid.

## Settlement to the creator

Razorpay settles eligible funds to the creator's registered bank account under its settlement terms.

## CloverLoop platform invoice

CloverLoop calculates applicable platform transaction commission on successful paid transactions and invoices the creator monthly. The commission is not deducted from the checkout payment.

## Separate fees

Razorpay processing fees are additional to CloverLoop platform charges. The creator should review both sets of charges separately.
