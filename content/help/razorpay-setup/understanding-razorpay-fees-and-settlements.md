Razorpay's payment processing charges are set by Razorpay and are separate from CloverLoop's published platform fees.

## Processing fees

Razorpay may charge processing fees and applicable taxes according to the creator's agreement and payment method. Check the current fee schedule in official Razorpay information or the account dashboard.

## Provider settlement

When using Razorpay independently, its settlement rules determine when eligible funds reach the bank account registered to that Razorpay account. The amount settled may differ from the original payment because of applicable fees, taxes, refunds, adjustments or other account entries.

## Settlement timing

Settlement schedules and processing times are governed by Razorpay and may vary by account, payment and risk checks. Use the Razorpay Dashboard for the latest status rather than relying on a general estimate.

## CloverLoop platform commission

Razorpay or another payment provider may charge processing fees under its own terms. Those charges are payable separately by the creator and are not included in CloverLoop's platform commission.
