## The short answer

CloverLoop is designed around creator storefronts for digital products. Its published plans are Free at ₹0/month with 0% on the first ₹10,000 Gross Sales per calendar month and 4% only on incremental Gross Sales above that allowance; Creator is ₹0/month at 4%; Pro Creator is ₹1,499/month at 2.5%. Payment-provider charges are separate from CloverLoop's platform commission.

Instamojo's [current official Online Store pricing page](https://www.instamojo.com/pricing/) lists Lite at 5% + ₹3, Starter at 5% + ₹3, and Growth at 2% + ₹3. Instamojo may suit sellers who need its broader ecommerce workflow, while CloverLoop's plans are focused on digital-product storefronts. ## Who should choose which platform?

Choose CloverLoop if you primarily sell ebooks, PDFs, study notes, templates, music downloads, presets or other digital files. Free offers a limited catalogue and a ₹10,000 monthly Gross Sales allowance. Creator supports unlimited products without a monthly subscription at 4%. Pro Creator charges ₹1,499/month at 2.5%.

Choose Instamojo if you also need a broader online-store workflow for physical goods, shipping or other ecommerce operations. Its current Online Store pricing lists percentage-plus-fixed fees and paid plan options; compare the tax and transaction details displayed for your own products and account.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does Instamojo currently list for transaction fees?

Its official Online Store pricing page lists Lite and Starter at 5% + ₹3, and Growth at 2% + ₹3. It says GST is charged in addition to those prices.

### Does Instamojo publish a separate current digital-product fee?

The official pricing page reviewed for this article shows Online Store plan rates rather than a separate digital-goods fee schedule. Confirm the fee for your product type in the current checkout.

### Does CloverLoop charge a fixed ₹3 per transaction?

No. CloverLoop's published rates are 0% on the first ₹10,000 in monthly Gross Sales on Free (4% only on incremental Gross Sales above the allowance), 4% on Creator and 2.5% on Pro Creator. Payment-provider processing charges are separate.

### Which platform is cheaper for a digital download?

It depends on the plan, monthly Gross Sales, product price, subscription and payment processing. Account for the Free-plan allowance, CloverLoop's separate provider charges, Instamojo's percentage-plus-fixed fee and applicable GST; no single plan is cheapest for every seller.
