This Privacy Policy explains how CloverLoop, operated by ARM Gear Co., collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal data when you visit the website, create an account, operate a storefront, browse a store, purchase a product or contact us.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

For account, platform, website, support, security and business-administration information, ARM Gear Co. determines purposes and means of processing and acts as the responsible entity / data fiduciary to the extent applicable law uses that concept. When a creator uses CloverLoop to receive and manage information about buyers, the creator may independently determine purposes for that information and CloverLoop may process it to provide storefront, order and delivery services. The precise role depends on the data and activity.

## 2. Information we may collect

### Creators and account holders

- Identity and contact details such as name, email, mobile number and account identifiers.
- Authentication and security information. Passwords should be stored by the authentication provider in protected form; CloverLoop does not need plaintext passwords.
- Storefront information such as username, profile, product titles, descriptions, prices, files, images and settings.
- Plan selection, subscription status, invoices, platform commission calculations and payment references.
- Razorpay account connection / integration information needed to associate a storefront with the creator's own payment account.
- Support requests, feedback, complaints and communications.

### Buyers and storefront visitors

- Information entered at checkout, which may include name, email and other details requested by the creator or needed to fulfil an order.
- Order details, product purchased, order status, download or access events, and payment references / transaction identifiers.
- Information submitted in forms or communications with the creator or CloverLoop.
- Technical information such as browser, device, approximate location derived from network information, referring page, pages viewed, timestamps and diagnostic logs, subject to applicable consent and configuration.

### Information from payment providers

Razorpay processes payment credentials such as card, UPI, bank and other payment information under its own systems and terms. CloverLoop should receive only transaction information and status needed to confirm an order, reconcile activity and support refunds or disputes. We do not intend to collect or store full card numbers, UPI PINs, banking passwords or payment authentication credentials.

## 3. Why we use information

- Create and administer accounts, authenticate users and operate creator storefronts.
- Display products, process order workflows, verify payment status and provide digital-product access or delivery.
- Calculate platform commissions, administer Pro Creator subscriptions, issue invoices and maintain business records.
- Send service notices, order confirmations, support responses and security alerts.
- Detect fraud, abuse, unauthorised access, policy violations and payment disputes.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, secure and improve the Platform and understand aggregate usage.
- Comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
- Send marketing communications only where we have an appropriate lawful basis or consent and provide an opt-out where required.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use buyer contact information for CloverLoop marketing merely because a buyer purchased from a creator.

## 4. Cookies, analytics and similar technologies

The Platform may use essential cookies or similar storage for authentication, security, session management, checkout and remembering privacy choices. Optional analytics or marketing technologies should remain disabled until the user makes the choice required by applicable law and our consent configuration. Exact technologies and cookie names depend on the live implementation and are described in the [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager may be used to measure website usage if configured and enabled.

## 5. Who we share information with

- Creators: a creator may receive buyer details and order information needed to fulfil a purchase, provide support and manage the creator's customer relationship.
- Supabase: may provide authentication, database and file-storage infrastructure, depending on deployed configuration.
- Vercel: may provide website hosting, content delivery, security and technical diagnostics.
- Resend: may provide transactional email delivery, such as account, order and product-delivery communications, if configured.
- Razorpay: processes buyer payments and provides payment status / transaction references under the creator's payment account and Razorpay's terms.
- Professional advisers and service providers: may receive limited information to provide accounting, legal, security or operational services under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
- Authorities and other parties: where disclosure is required by law, court order or regulation, or necessary to protect rights, safety, users or the Platform.

Providers may process information in India or other countries depending on infrastructure and settings.

## 6. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, order fulfilment, support, security, dispute handling, legal compliance and tax / accounting records. Retention periods vary by data type and legal requirement. When information is no longer required, we will delete it, anonymise it or securely restrict access, subject to backups, audit logs and legal holds.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. These may include encrypted connections, access controls, authentication protections, database permissions, restricted administrative access, backups and monitoring. No online service can guarantee absolute security. We will handle and notify affected users and authorities of personal-data breaches as required by applicable law.

## 8. Your choices and rights

Depending on the law applicable to you and commencement of relevant provisions, you may have rights to request access to information about your personal data, correction, updating, erasure, withdrawal of consent, grievance redressal and nomination of another person in specified circumstances. You may unsubscribe from non-essential marketing. Send requests to mycloverbase@gmail.com. We may verify your identity and retain information where legally required or an exception applies.

Buyers may contact the creator about information the creator controls in connection with the creator's store. You may also contact CloverLoop about Platform-related processing or if you are unsure whom to contact.

## 9. Children

Creator accounts are intended for individuals aged 18 or older. A person under 18 should not independently create a creator account. Buyers under 18 should use the Platform only with parent or legal guardian involvement and permission where required. If we become aware that personal data has been collected contrary to applicable child-protection requirements, we will take appropriate steps.

## 10. Third-party websites and services

Creator storefronts may link to external websites, files or services. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms. CloverLoop does not control their data practices.

## 11. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy as the Platform, providers or legal requirements change. We will update the date above and provide notice where required.
