CloverLoop offers three plans. The plan determines the platform transaction fee and the number of paid and free product listings available to the creator.

## Plan comparison

Free: ₹0 monthly subscription, 0% platform transaction fee, one paid product listing and five free product listings per account. Creator: ₹0 monthly subscription, 3% platform transaction fee and unlimited paid and free product listings. Pro Creator: ₹10,000 monthly subscription, 2% platform transaction fee and unlimited paid and free product listings.

## What the transaction fee applies to

Platform transaction commission applies to successful paid transactions. Free products do not incur platform transaction commission.

## How CloverLoop bills

CloverLoop calculates applicable commission monthly and invoices the creator separately. The commission is not deducted from the customer's checkout payment.

## Razorpay fees are separate

Razorpay processing charges are additional and governed by the creator's Razorpay account terms.

## Creator plan example

If monthly successful paid sales total ₹1,00,000, a 3% platform transaction fee equals ₹3,000. This excludes Razorpay processing fees.

## Pro Creator example

If monthly successful paid sales total ₹5,00,000, a 2% platform transaction fee equals ₹10,000. Add the ₹10,000 monthly subscription. Total CloverLoop charges are ₹20,000, excluding Razorpay processing fees.

## Choosing a plan

Compare expected paid sales and the listing limits you need. Consider the monthly subscription and expected sales, not only the headline percentage.
