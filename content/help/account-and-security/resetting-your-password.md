Use the password recovery option on the CloverLoop login page if you cannot remember your password.

## Request a reset

Enter the email address associated with your account and submit the reset request.

## Open the email

Use the password reset link sent to your inbox. Check spam or junk folders if it does not arrive.

## Choose a new password

Create a unique password that you do not use on other websites. Do not share the reset link.

## If the link fails

Request a new reset email and use the latest link. Contact CloverLoop support if the issue continues.
