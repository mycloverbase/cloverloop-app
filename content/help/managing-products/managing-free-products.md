Free products can help creators share samples, introductory resources or other downloads with their audience. A free product has a price of zero and does not attract platform transaction commission.

## Free plan limit

The Free plan's published allowance is up to five free product listings per account. This is a limit on the number of listings, not the number of downloads.

## Creator and Pro Creator

Creator and Pro Creator plan terms include unlimited free product listings.

## Free products and commission

CloverLoop does not charge platform transaction commission on free products.

## When the limit is reached

Review the published plan terms for listing limits. For questions about the options shown in your account, contact CloverLoop support.
