CloverLoop publishes three plans. The plan determines the platform transaction fee and the number of paid and free product listings available to the creator.

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Monthly price | Platform transaction fee | Product listings and Gross Sales |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | ₹0 | 0% up to the limit | 1 paid product and 5 free products per account lifetime; ₹10,000 Gross Sales per calendar month |
| Creator | ₹0 | 4% | Unlimited paid and free products; no monthly Gross Sales limit |
| Pro Creator | ₹2,999 | 0% | Unlimited paid and free products; no monthly Gross Sales limit |

Free has no CloverLoop platform transaction fee up to ₹10,000 Gross Sales per calendar month. Reaching that limit requires an upgrade to Creator to continue selling; an upgrade requires the creator's confirmation. Creator has a 4% platform transaction fee, and Pro Creator has a 0% platform transaction fee with a ₹2,999 monthly subscription. Platform rates and monthly subscription prices are exclusive of applicable GST where required.

## Payment provider charges

Razorpay or another payment provider may charge processing fees under its own terms. Those charges are payable separately by the creator and are not included in CloverLoop's platform commission.

The Pro Creator subscription is not available until CloverLoop configures and verifies a payment provider. No subscription will be activated or charged based only on a browser redirect.

## Choosing a plan

Free is the zero-price entry point for a small catalogue, with no platform transaction fee up to ₹10,000 Gross Sales per calendar month and one paid and five free product listings per account lifetime. Creator is the no-monthly-fee option for unlimited listings at a 4% platform fee and no monthly Gross Sales limit. Pro Creator is intended to cost ₹2,999/month and have no platform transaction fee, but subscriptions are not yet available until CloverLoop configures a payment provider.

## Founding Creator Programme

The programme is an entitlement overlay on Creator, not a separate paid plan. The first 50 creators who register through [the founder referral link](https://cloverloop.in/signup?ref=founder) and successfully publish their storefront may receive six calendar months of unlimited Creator listings with a 0% platform fee. Signup alone does not qualify; product publication alone does not qualify. After the programme expires, Creator membership and unlimited listings continue at the standard 4% platform fee. CloverLoop does not start a subscription automatically.
