Use the sign-in method associated with your CloverLoop account. If Google authentication is available, choose the Google option and complete the Google sign-in process.

## Email login

Enter the email address and password associated with your account.

## Google login

Select Google sign-in and choose the correct Google account. Complete any authentication steps shown.

## Authentication problems

Check that you are using the correct account and that your browser permits the required authentication redirects. If the problem persists, use password recovery if applicable or contact CloverLoop support.
