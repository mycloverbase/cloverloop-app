When planning to share a creator storefront, make it easy for the right audience to understand what they will find and why it may be useful.

## Instagram

Plan a concise profile description and identify where a storefront link would be most useful to interested followers.

## YouTube

Decide which videos and channel pages are relevant to your offer. Explain how each product relates to the content.

## WhatsApp

Share information with people who have asked about the product or opted to receive updates. Avoid unsolicited bulk messaging.

## Website and newsletter

Consider a clear Store or Digital Products section in your website navigation and relevant newsletter content.

## Set clear expectations

Tell people what they will find when they open the link. Use accurate descriptions and do not promise content that is not included.
