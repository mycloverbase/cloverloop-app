If you no longer want to use CloverLoop, contact CloverLoop support to request account deletion.

## Before requesting deletion

Review outstanding customer obligations, active products, payment records and invoices. Retain information you are entitled to keep before deletion.

## Understand the effect

Account deletion may affect access to account information and any associated records. Review the applicable policies and contact CloverLoop support if you need details about your request.

## Confirm the request

Follow the confirmation steps shown by CloverLoop. Do not assume deleted information can be recovered.
