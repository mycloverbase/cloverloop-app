Protect your CloverLoop account, your email account and your connected payment account. Anyone who gains access to these accounts may be able to view sensitive information or perform actions in your name.

## Use unique credentials

Use a strong password that is not reused on other services.

## Protect verification codes

Never share one-time passwords, recovery codes or authentication links.

## Use official websites

Access CloverLoop and Razorpay through their official websites. Be cautious of messages asking you to sign in through unfamiliar links.

## Protect payment credentials

Do not send Razorpay passwords, secret keys or other confidential credentials through email, chat or support requests.

## Review account access

If you suspect unauthorised access, secure your email account and payment account and contact the relevant provider.
