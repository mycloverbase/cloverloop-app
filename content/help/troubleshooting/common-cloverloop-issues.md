Use these checks for account access and plan questions. If you need help, contact CloverLoop support with a clear description and relevant non-sensitive references.

## I cannot sign up or log in

Check the email address, password and verification status. For Google sign-in, confirm that you selected the intended Google account. Use password recovery if needed.

## I cannot find a storefront, product or payment option

Review the platform availability notice on your signed-in dashboard and use the options shown in your account. Contact CloverLoop support if you need clarification.

## I have a question about plan listing limits

Review the published plan terms for paid and free product listing allowances. Contact support if you need help understanding how a plan applies to your account.

## I have a payment or settlement question

For a transaction handled by an external payment provider, check that provider's records and support guidance.

## What should I send to support?

Provide a concise description, time of issue, relevant order or product reference and exact non-sensitive error message. Never send passwords, OTPs, secret keys or full payment credentials.
